# Portfolio

Mohamed Saleh's personal site: it proves, to hiring managers and prospective clients, that he builds real systems, and the site itself is evidence of his craft.

## Language

### Work

**Project**:
A piece of shipped work Mohamed presents on the site — usually a system built for a client or employer. Every Project is either a Featured Project or listed in the Archive.
_Avoid_: Repo, app, product, item

**Case Study**:
The dedicated page for one Project, explaining the problem, Mohamed's role, the decisions he made and the outcome.
_Avoid_: Project page, detail page, post

**Featured Project**:
One of the Projects (True Legal, Kidzenia, Tiba Masr, Aqsat, True Visor) shown prominently in the Work section, each with a full Case Study. KTC Scanner Hub is paused until its Case Study can be written.
_Avoid_: Highlight, top project

**Archive**:
The index of every other Project worth mentioning, one line each with no Case Study, such as WakeUp, the Odoo implementation and Tick Hub.
_Avoid_: Other work, misc, side projects

**Under the Hood**:
The part of a Case Study that exposes the real technical inside of a Project (sync engine, auth, offline queue, data flow), for Visitors who want to look past the screens.
_Avoid_: Tech details, behind the scenes

**Playground**:
A separate, optional page of interactive experiments, linked from the footer, kept away from the paths a recruiter follows.
_Avoid_: Lab (reserved for the design preview pages), sandbox, fun page

### Experience

**Visitor**:
Anyone viewing the site — a recruiter, an engineering manager, a prospective client or a fellow developer.
_Avoid_: User (reserved for the people who use Mohamed's Projects)

**Signature Moment**:
The single deliberately spectacular, WebGL-driven interaction the site is remembered by. Everything else stays restrained.
_Avoid_: Hero effect, wow section

**Reduced Motion Experience**:
The complete, still-designed version of the site served to Visitors who ask for less motion — not a broken fallback.
_Avoid_: Fallback, static mode
