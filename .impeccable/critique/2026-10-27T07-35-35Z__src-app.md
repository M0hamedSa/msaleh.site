## Palette
1. Light is warm (bone h45); dark is cold steel (h216); the accent shifts to iOS systemRed #ff3b30.
2. Red has about 15 roles. Non-live statuses (Pilot, Launching) get red dots, so they read as broken. The pulsing red "Open to select projects" dot reads as an alert.
3. The off-system #3ddc84 live dot is 1.48:1 on bone.
4. The screen treatment is sepia in light and glares white in dark. Brand colours (True Legal's orange-red) clash with the accent red.
